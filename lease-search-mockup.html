Standard Commercial Lease Agreement
La Jolla Commons Property Owner, LLC — Executed Instrument

This Lease Agreement ("Lease") is entered into as of the Effective Date by and between La Jolla Commons Property Owner, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("Landlord"), and Coastal Retail Group LLC, a California limited liability company ("Tenant"), for the premises located at La Jolla Commons, Suite 214, San Diego, CA 92037 ("Premises"), as more particularly described in Exhibit A attached hereto.

WHEREAS, Landlord is the owner of the building commonly known as La Jolla Commons; and WHEREAS, Tenant desires to lease the Premises for the Permitted Use set forth in Section 3 below, the parties agree to the terms set forth herein.

Section 9 — Maintenance & Repairs

Landlord shall be responsible for maintenance and repair of common areas, structural elements, roof, and loading dock facilities serving the Premises, except where such condition arises from Tenant's negligence or misuse. Tenant shall provide written notice of any condition requiring repair, including water intrusion or damage, within forty-eight (48) hours of discovery, delivered to Landlord's designated notice address set forth in Section 14. Failure to provide timely notice may limit Tenant's remedies under this Section.

Section 14 — Notices

All notices, demands, or other communications under this Lease shall be in writing and delivered to Tenant at: Attn: Facilities Manager, Coastal Retail Group LLC, notices@coastalretailgroup.com. Notices delivered by email shall be deemed received on the date sent, provided a read receipt or confirmation is obtained; notices delivered by mail shall be deemed received three (3) business days after deposit.

Section 22 — Insurance

Tenant shall maintain commercial general liability insurance in an amount not less than $2,000,000 per occurrence, with Landlord named as additional insured, evidenced by a current Certificate of Insurance on file with Landlord at all times during the Term. Tenant shall furnish a renewal certificate no later than thirty (30) days prior to expiration of any such policy.
