Section 22 — Insurance

Tenant shall, at its sole cost, maintain in full force during the Term commercial general liability insurance with limits of not less than $2,000,000 per occurrence and $4,000,000 in the general aggregate.

Landlord, Landlord's property manager, and any mortgagee designated by Landlord shall be named as additional insureds on Tenant's commercial general liability policy, evidenced by an endorsement attached to the Certificate of Insurance.

The Certificate of Insurance shall list Landlord as certificate holder at the notice address set forth in Section 14, including Tenant's specific suite designation, and shall be kept current with Landlord's property management office at all times during the Term.

Tenant shall maintain property insurance on a replacement cost basis covering Tenant's personal property, trade fixtures, and any Tenant-installed improvements within the Premises.

Tenant shall maintain workers' compensation insurance in statutory amounts as required under California law for all employees working at the Premises.
